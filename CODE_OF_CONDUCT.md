# Code of conduct

NEXUS should be a project anyone can use and contribute to without being treated badly. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place the project is represented.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source, to the codebase or to English.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Explain the why.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and say no respectfully yourself.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Unwanted sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated unwelcome contact.
- Spam, including promotional posts and automated or AI-generated contributions submitted without review by a person.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the e-mail address on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/yazhiniyanMS), or through a [private security report](https://github.com/yazhiniyanMS/nexus/security/advisories/new) if it is urgent. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban people who do not follow this code, in proportion to what happened.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
